## Planetary maps

Solar System Scope / INOVE produced these equirectangular maps using planetary imagery and elevation data. They supplement unmapped areas and adjust colors for visual clarity. SOLARIS resized the maps and encoded them as JPEGs. The maps retain their Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license. ## Saturn portrait

`web/public/images/saturn-portrait.webp` derives from *Jewel of the Solar System*, PIA17474, a natural-color Cassini mosaic acquired on 10 October 2013. Image credit **NASA/JPL-Caltech/SSI/Cornell**. SOLARIS resized the original and converted it to WebP. [Original image and caption](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia17474-jewel-of-the-solar-system/) · [JPL image use policy](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/)

`web/public/images/saturn-wide.webp` derives from *The Greatest Saturn Portrait ...Yet*, PIA06193, photographed by Cassini in October 2004. Image credit **NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute**. SOLARIS rotated the image 23 degrees, trimmed its black border, reduced its dimensions, and encoded it as WebP. [Original image and caption](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia06193-the-greatest-saturn-portrait-yet/) · [JPL image use policy](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/)

## Saturn ring profile

`saturn-rings.png` is Solar System Scope / INOVE's 2048 × 125 RGBA ring texture, distributed here unchanged under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. Its horizontal axis runs from the inner edge at left to the outer edge at right. The darker gap between the bright B ring and the A ring appears about two thirds of the way across. [Source image](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_saturn_ring_alpha.png) · [Texture collection](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
